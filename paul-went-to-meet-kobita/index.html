Paul went to meet Kobita

Paul went to meet Kobita and her family the next day. He was in his blue trousers and a grey T-shirt. Kobita’s mother, Ms Shahana was a shy woman and she was reluctant to come in front of Paul. But Paul greeted her warmly in his newly learnt Bangla, “Kemon achen?”. Ms Shahana loved hearing a foreigner speak in Bangla. She welcomed Paul to her house. Kobita started talking to Paul. She wanted him to write something for her school magazine. As they were talking. Ms Shahana prepared quite a number of Bangladeshi dishes. She served him lunch at noon. Paul had plain rice, chicken curry, fish bhuna, dal and salad for lunch. He liked the tastes of all those delicious items except the dried fish bhorta. He is afraid of hot chillies. He also does not like eating fresh onions. So he skipped the mashed potato which had chopped fresh onions in it. But Paul became very fond of the dessert made of rice, gur, milk and coconut. He also enjoyed drinking tea made of fresh cow milk. Paul thanked Kobita and Ms Shahana for the lunch. In fact, he was tired of having vegetables and fried eggs for the last few weeks. He hates eating the same food for weeks. So he enjoyed the tastes of a variety of Bangladeshi dishes.

তারাকথাবলারসময়, মিসেসশাহানাঅনেকগুলোবাংলাদেশিখাবারপ্রস্তুতকরলেন।তিনিদুপুরেপলকেদুপুরেরখাবারপরিবেশনকরলেন।পলভাত, মুরগিরকারি, মাছভুনা, ডালএবংসালাদখেল।সেসবসুস্বাদুখাবারেরস্বাদপছন্দকরল, শুধুশুকনোমাছেরভর্তাছাড়া।সেঝালমরিচেভয়পায়।তাছাড়াসেকাঁচাপেঁয়াজখেতেপছন্দকরেনা।তাইসেআলুভর্তাখায়নি, কারণতাতেকাঁচাপেঁয়াজকুচিছিল।কিন্তুপলখুবপছন্দকরলএকটিমিষ্টান্নযাচাল, গুড়, দুধওনারকেলদিয়েতৈরিছিল।সেগরুরদুধদিয়েবানানোচাওখেয়েখুবউপভোগকরল।

  1. Paul went to meet Kobita and her family the next day. He was excited to visit them and see his friend.
  2. He wore blue trousers and a grey T-shirt. His outfit was simple and comfortable for the visit.
  3. Ms. Shahana is Kobita’s mother. She is a shy woman who feels a little nervous around strangers.
  4. Ms. Shahana felt reluctant to come in front of Paul. She was not used to meeting foreigners.
  5. Paul greeted her warmly in Bangla. He said, “Kemon achen?” which made her feel happy and respected.
  6. He had recently learned to speak Bangla. Ms. Shahana loved hearing a foreigner speak her language.
  7. Ms. Shahana welcomed Paul into the house. She was pleased with his polite and friendly behaviour.
  8. Kobita started talking to Paul first. She wanted to involve him in her school magazine work.
  9. Kobita asked Paul to write something for her school magazine. She hoped he would help with her project.
  10. While they were talking, Ms. Shahana prepared several Bangladeshi dishes. The food smelled delicious and inviting.
  11. Ms. Shahana served Paul lunch at noon. She wanted him to enjoy traditional Bangladeshi meals.
  12. Paul had plain rice, chicken curry, fish bhuna, dal, and salad. He tried a little of everything on the table.
  13. He liked the taste of all the dishes except dried fish bhorta. The spiciness of the dish was too strong for him.
  14. Paul is afraid of hot chillies. He avoids foods that are very spicy.
  15. He also does not like eating fresh onions. Because of this, he skipped the mashed potato with chopped onions.
  16. Paul became very fond of the dessert made of rice, gur, milk, and coconut. It was sweet and delicious.
  17. He also enjoyed drinking tea made from fresh cow milk. The tea was smooth and tasty.
  18. Paul thanked Kobita and Ms. Shahana for the lunch. He appreciated their kindness and hospitality.
  19. He had been eating vegetables and fried eggs for the last few weeks. He was tired of the same simple food.
  20. Paul hates eating the same food for weeks. He prefers variety and different flavours in his meals.
  21. He enjoyed the tastes of a variety of Bangladeshi dishes. The food was new, exciting, and delicious for him.
  22. Paul appreciated the care and effort put into the meal. He felt happy and satisfied after eating.
  23. He tried all the dishes carefully. Even though he skipped some items, he enjoyed the overall experience.
  24. The dessert made a strong impression on him. Its sweetness and flavour were very appealing.
  25. Drinking fresh milk tea was refreshing for Paul. It complemented the meal perfectly.
  26. Paul felt very welcome in Kobita’s house. He enjoyed spending time with her and her family.
  27. He liked experiencing traditional Bangladeshi cuisine. The variety of dishes gave him a sense of the local culture.
  28. Paul’s visit was enjoyable and memorable. The hospitality and food made him feel special and happy.
  29. He learned to appreciate different tastes and flavours. The experience made his day very pleasant.
  30. Paul left the meal feeling grateful. He thanked everyone once again before saying goodbye.
