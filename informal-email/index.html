3. E-mail to your friendabout Bangladesh and her people

From     : tonika123@gmail.com

To          : mithila123@gmail.com

Subject: About Bangladesh and her people.

Dear Mithila,

I received your e-mail yesterday. You have wanted to know about Bangladesh and her people. Bangladesh is a small country with an area of 1,47,570 square kilometers. It became independent through a sanguinary war in 1971. It is a tropical country. It has six seasons. It is called a land of rivers. Our land is very fertile. The monsoon blows over it and there is a lot of rain here. About 80 percent of people live in the villages. Most of the people are Muslims. There are also Hindu, Buddhist and Christian communities in our country. We also have Tribal people who have their own language and culture. The hospitality of our people is world famous. Rice and fish is our main food. We entertain other people with sweets when we have good news. Males wear lungi and women wear a sari. People observe various kinds of festivals gorgeously almost all the year round. Pahela Baishakh is the biggest celebration in which all the people join. Bangladesh is known as the darling child of nature. It is a country of scenic beauty. The Sundarbans, the forests of hilly districts, the tea gardens of Sylhet and Cox’s Bazar sea beach etc. are the important tourist attractions of Bangladesh. 

We are proud of our country, our culture and heritage. You are cordially invited to visit our country. Hope to hear from you soon. I am fine.

Your friend,

Tonika

13. Suppose your friend lives in Australia. She has heard about the largest mangrove forest of the world, the Sundarbans. Now write an email asking her to visit Bangladesh after the pandemic.

The Sundarbans is a vast forest on the coast of the Bay of Bengal. It is located in both Bangladesh and West Bengal of India. It is one of the natural wonders of the world. The full forest is covered with a crisscrossed network of lake or river. Not only for the forest but also, its ecological systems make it one of the best natural beauty in the world. Mangrove forest, spotted deer, wild bears, Royal Bengal Tiger, Crocodile, Monkey and many other wild animals altogether make suburban a real threat of wildlife. This forest is unique for Sundari tree species and the Royal Bengal tiger. It was declared a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 1987. Numerous tourists from local country and abroad are fascinated by the unique beauty of the Sundarbans. I highly request you to visit Bangladesh after the pandemic and to enjoy the God gifted unique beauty in your own eyes.
