Hello, I am Kobita

Hello, I am Kobita. I am a class 7 student of Sundarpur High School. I like listening to music in my free time. I like folk songs but I don’t understand classical music that much. However, my father likes classical music very much, bur he doesn’t like pop music. He never warches pop musical programmes on TV. He says he hates them because they don’t appeal to emotions. My favourite singer is Farida Parvın. I like her songs very much. She has a wonderful voice. I also like gardening. I have planted many flowers in my garden. When the flowers are in full bloom, I love to watch them. I’m very happy about the flowers that I grow. I just adore them!

হ্যালো, আমিকবিতা।আমিসুন্দরপুরউচ্চবিদ্যালয়েরসপ্তমশ্রেণিরছাত্রী।অবসরসময়েআমিগানশুনতেভালোবাসি।আমিলোকগানপছন্দকরি, তবেশাস্ত্রীয়সংগীততেমনভাবেবুঝিনা।তবেআমারবাবাশাস্ত্রীয়সংগীতখুবপছন্দকরেন, কিন্তুতিনিপপসংগীতপছন্দকরেননা।তিনিকখনওটিভিতেপপসংগীতেরঅনুষ্ঠানদেখেননা।তিনিবলেন, তিনিএসবগানঅপছন্দকরেনকারণএগুলোমানুষেরঅনুভূতিতেছোঁয়াদেয়না।

আমারপ্রিয়গায়িকাফরিদাপারভীন।আমিতারগানখুবইপছন্দকরি।তারকণ্ঠস্বরঅত্যন্তসুন্দর।আমিবাগানকরাখুবভালোবাসি।আমিআমারবাগানেঅনেকফুললাগিয়েছি।যখনফুলগুলোপূর্ণপ্রস্ফুটিতহয়, আমিসেগুলোদেখতেখুবভালোবাসি।আমিআমারলাগানোফুলগুলোনিয়েখুবখুশি।আমিসত্যিইতাদেরভীষণভালোবাসি!

Answer

  1. Kobita is a student of class 7 at Sundarpur High School. She is a cheerful girl who loves music and nature.
  2. She studies at Sundarpur High School, a well-known school in her village. She enjoys learning and spending time with her friends there.
  3. In her free time, she likes to listen to music. It helps her feel relaxed and happy.
  4. She enjoys folk songs because they reflect the traditions of her country. She listens to them often at home.
  5. Classical music is hard for her to understand. She finds it too slow and complicated.
  6. Her father loves classical music very much. He often listens to it in his leisure time.
  7. He dislikes pop music. He says it doesn’t touch the emotions of the listeners.
  8. He never watches pop musical programmes on TV. He thinks they are meaningless and noisy.
  9. Farida Parvin is Kobita’s favourite singer. She admires her sweet and melodious voice.
  10. Kobita believes Farida Parvin has a wonderful voice. She listens to her songs almost every day.
  11. Besides music, Kobita loves gardening. She spends her free mornings watering her plants.
  12. She has planted many flowers in her garden. They make her home beautiful and colourful.
  13. When the flowers are in full bloom, she feels very happy. She enjoys watching them sway in the breeze.
  14. The flowers in her garden make her proud. They are a result of her care and love.
  15. She adores all the flowers she grows. They are like her little friends in nature.
  16. Kobita feels proud of her hobbies. Both music and gardening bring her peace of mind.
  17. Her father and she have different tastes in music. He prefers classical songs while she loves folk music.
  18. Kobita spends time in her garden every day. It keeps her close to nature.
  19. Her father says pop music doesn’t appeal to human emotions. He prefers songs that touch the heart.
  20. Kobita’s favourite songs are by Farida Parvin. They make her feel calm and inspired.
  21. She feels proud when her garden is full of flowers. It shows her hard work and care.
  22. Her flowers bloom beautifully in different colours. They attract butterflies and birds.
  23. Kobita’s father never enjoys pop songs. He believes classical music is more meaningful.
  24. Kobita feels that folk songs express real life. They tell stories of people and their emotions.
  25. She always keeps her garden neat and clean. It is her favourite place to spend time.
  26. Music and gardening are two things that make her happy. They help her feel peaceful and creative.
  27. Her father listens to classical songs every evening. It reminds him of traditional Bangla culture.
  28. Kobita loves listening to music before going to bed. It makes her feel calm and relaxed.
  29. She enjoys both listening to music and taking care of flowers. These are the things that make her life beautiful.
