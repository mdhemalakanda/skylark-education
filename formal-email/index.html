1. An email to a bookseller to send some books

To         : hr.skylarkpublicationltd@gmail.com

Subject: To send some books.

Dear Sir,

I am an ardent reader of books published by your publishing company. All the books have excellent content, are written by top notch writers/authors and are excellent in quality. I tried to look out for the books in the local bookstore in my city as well as online but couldn’t find them. Hence I plan to buy the books directly through you through VPP. I shall be highly obliged if you kindly send me the following books per V.P.P. to the address given below at your earliest convenience. I enclose herewith a check (No. A4304) for 5000/- as advance.

List of books:

a. Skylark English Dictionary (5 Copies)

b. Skylark Bengali Dictionary (2 Copies)

c. Skylark General English for HSC (2 Copies)

d. Skylark Elective English for HSC (2 Copies)

e. Skylark Punjabi Guide (Compulsory) for HSC (2 Copies)

f. Skylark Recurrent Essays and Current Affairs (2 Copies)

g. Kindly treat this order as urgent.

Yours faithfully,

Name….

Address…

3. E mail to manager of a library to know the process of becoming a member

From     : Al Kariakaria@yahoo.com

To          : Asm Haqueasm@yahoo.com

Sent      : Thursday, March 20, 2014; 12:09 pm

Subject  : to know the process of becoming a member of the library

Dear Sir,

Being a studious student and curious to know, I have decided to involve myself in your esteemed and enriched library. But I am fully unaware of the proceeding about what I have to fulfill to become a proud member and enjoy all the services on offer of your library. Please help to let me know how to join the library. How much time will it take to issue me a library card? How much should I pay? What can I do with my card? Is there a charge for borrowing items? How long can I keep items? What happens if I lose or damage an item? What happens if I don’t return items on time? What happens if my card is lost, stolen or damaged? What do I need to do if I change address? What do I need to do if I change my email address?

I will be highly pleased if you inform me above mentioned questions as early as possible.

Sincerely yours

Subject : For repairing damaged roads.

Dear Sir,

I would like to inform you that I am an inhabitant of the village Narayanpur in Ghorshal Union in the district of Jhenaidah. I would like to draw your kind attention to the fact that the main road of our village has been badly damaged by the devastating flood recently. Consequently, water remains here and there. It is really impossible for us to go along the damaged roads. Students as well as the people of our area are the worst sufferers. People often fall down and get drenched.

I hope that your honour would be kind enough to take necessary steps to repair the damaged roads, especially the main road first so that we, all the inhabitants of Narayanpur, can move comfortably. I would really appreciate your favour regarding the issue.

Best regards,

Md. Abdur Rashid,

17. Write an email to know admission procedure

To        : oxfordadmission@gmail.com

Subject: Want to know about Admission Procedure

Sir,

This is to request more information concerning English Language and Literature at Oxford University. I am extremely interested in this course. Therefore, I would like to inquire about some information. First, could you let me know whether HSC is one of your accepted qualifications? If so, I would be grateful if you could point to the academic entry requirements of the University of Oxford for Bangladeshi students. 

Second, I would like to express my sincere appreciation if you could indicate some information about the English Language requirements for an applicant from a non-English speaking country like Bangladesh and, if accepted and enrolled, would you classify me as a home student or as an international student for fees purposes? Finally, it would really help me out if you could indicate whether I would be eligible for any scholarship.

I shall be highly obliged if you, send me a catalogue mentioning the admission procedure, tuition fees, school rules etc at your earliest convenience.

Yours sincerely
