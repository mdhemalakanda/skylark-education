Literature

A Little Plant

In the heart of a seed 

Buried deep, so deep,

A dear little plant       

Lay fast asleep.

“Wake!” said the voice

Of the rain drops bright.

The little plant heard.

And rose to see

What the wonderful

Outside world might be.

একটিছোটগাছ

একটিবীজেরহৃদয়ে
গভীর, খুবগভীরে
একটিপ্রিয়ছোটগাছ
ছিলঘুমিয়েচুপচাপ।

“জাগো!” — বললকণ্ঠস্বর
উজ্জ্বলবৃষ্টিরফোঁটা।
ছোটগাছটিশুনলসেইডাকে,
আরউঠেএলদেখতে
বাহিরেরআশ্চর্য
দুনিয়াটিকেমনহতেপারে।

Theme: The poem illustrates the miracle of growth and awakening in nature. It symbolizes the journey of life beginning from a small, hidden place and being encouraged by natural forces like rain. The message is one of hope, discovery, and the beauty of new beginnings.

থিম: এই কবিতাটি প্রকৃতির জাগরণ ও বৃদ্ধির আশ্চর্য ঘটনাকে তুলে ধরে। এটি একটি ছোট বীজ থেকে জীবনের সূচনা এবং বৃষ্টির আহ্বানে তার জাগরণের প্রতীক। কবিতাটি আশা, আবিষ্কার ও নতুন শুরুর সৌন্দর্যের বার্তা দেয়।

Summary: The poem tells the story of a tiny plant hidden deep inside a seed. It lies asleep, unaware of the outside world. One day, the bright raindrops call out to it. The little plant hears the voice and awakens. It rises up to see the wonders of the world beyond the soil.

সারাংশ: এইকবিতায়একটিছোটগাছেরকথাবলাহয়েছে, যাএকটিবীজেরগভীরেলুকিয়েঘুমিয়েছিল।বাইরেরজগতসম্পর্কেতারকোনোধারণাছিলনা।একদিনউজ্জ্বলবৃষ্টিরফোঁটাতাকেডেকেতোলে।ছোটগাছটিসেইডাকশুনেজেগেওঠে।তারপরসেমাটিরবাইরেউঠেআশ্চর্যসুন্দরপৃথিবীকেদেখতেচায়।

  1. Who is the poem about?
    The poem is about a little plant. It is buried deep inside a seed, sleeping.
  2. Who was sleeping in the seed?
    A little plant was sleeping inside the seed. It was hidden deep beneath the surface.
  3. Who called the little plant?
    The bright raindrops called the little plant. They encouraged it to wake up.
  4. Who heard the voice of the rain?
    The little plant heard the rain’s voice. This voice made it rise.
  5. Who woke the little plant?
    The raindrops woke the little plant. Their call made it curious about the outside world.
  6. Who wanted to see the outside world?
    The little plant wanted to see the outside world. It rose from the seed to discover it.
  7. Who lay fast asleep in the seed?
    The dear little plant lay fast asleep. It was deeply buried and unaware.
  8. Who is the poet talking about?
    The poet is talking about the life of a tiny plant. It begins its journey from deep within a seed.
  9. Who represents hope and new life in the poem?
    The little plant represents hope and new life. It symbolizes a new beginning.
  10. Who responded to the rain’s voice?
    The plant responded to the rain’s call. It woke up and began to grow.
  11. Where was the little plant sleeping?
    The little plant was sleeping in the heart of a seed. It was buried very deep.
  12. Where did the rain fall?
    The rain fell outside the seed. Its voice reached the plant inside.
  13. Where did the little plant rise to?
    The little plant rose from beneath the soil. It came up to see the outside world.
  14. Where was the seed buried?
    The seed was buried deep in the earth. It was a quiet and hidden place.
  15. Where did the plant live before waking?
    It lived inside the heart of the seed. It was asleep in darkness.
  16. Where did the voice of the raindrops come from?
    The voice came from the world above the ground. It was the natural call of rain.
  17. Where did the little plant begin its journey?
    The journey began in the seed. From there, it moved upward toward the surface.
  18. Where did the plant hear the voice?
    The plant heard the voice while still buried. The sound reached it from above.
  19. Where did the plant go after waking up?
    It rose to the surface of the soil. There, it saw the outside world.
  20. Where was the “wonderful world” mentioned in the poem?
    The wonderful world was outside the seed. It was waiting for the plant to discover.
  21. When was the little plant asleep?
    It was asleep while buried deep in the seed. This was before the rain came.
  22. When did the rain speak to the plant?
    The rain spoke when it began to fall. Its voice was bright and encouraging.
  23. When did the plant wake up?
    The plant woke up after hearing the rain. The sound made it curious and active.
  24. When did the plant rise from the seed?
    It rose after waking up from its deep sleep. This marked the beginning of its life.
  25. When does the journey of growth begin?
    It begins when the plant hears the rain’s voice. That moment inspires the plant to grow.
  26. When was the plant buried?
    The plant was buried before the poem begins. It was deep inside the seed.
  27. When did the outside world become visible to the plant?
    After the plant rose from the soil. Then it could see the world above ground.
  28. When does the poem take place?
    It takes place during the beginning of a plant’s life. Likely during a rainfall.
  29. When did the rain inspire life?
    The rain inspired life when it called to the sleeping plant. Its voice brought awakening.
  30. When did the plant begin to grow?
    The plant began to grow after hearing the rain. It rose to meet the world.
  31. What was hidden in the heart of the seed?
    A little plant was hidden there. It lay sleeping, deep within.
  32. What was the plant doing at first?
    The plant was fast asleep. It was unaware of the world.
  33. What woke the little plant?
    The voice of the bright raindrops woke it. Their sound reached deep into the seed.
  34. What did the plant want to see?
    The plant wanted to see the outside world. It rose to explore what was above.
  35. What does the poem show about nature?
    The poem shows that nature awakens gently. Life begins with care and wonder.
  36. What is the mood of the poem?
    The mood is gentle, hopeful, and full of wonder. It celebrates new beginnings.
  37. What does the rain represent in the poem?
    The rain represents encouragement and life. It awakens growth.
  38. What does the plant symbolize?
    The plant symbolizes potential and new life. It stands for growth and discovery.
  39. What kind of world does the plant see?
    It sees a wonderful world. This world is full of light and beauty.
  40. What lesson can we learn from the poem?
    We learn that every small life has beauty and purpose. Growth begins quietly but powerfully.
  41. Why was the plant sleeping?
    It was not yet ready to grow. It waited for the right time and condition.
  42. Why did the rain call the plant?
    The rain came to nourish and awaken it. It was time for life to begin.
  43. Why did the plant rise?
    It rose because it heard the rain’s call. The voice encouraged it to grow.
  44. Why did the plant want to see the outside world?
    The outside world was new and exciting. It was drawn by curiosity and wonder.
  45. Why did the poet describe the plant as “dear”?
    Because the plant is small, precious, and full of potential. It evokes care and affection.
  46. Why is the seed described as being “deep”?
    To show that the plant was well hidden. It emphasizes how far it had to grow.
  47. Why is the poem hopeful?
    Because it shows life awakening with gentle encouragement. It celebrates new beginnings.
  48. Why does the poet use simple language?
    To match the innocence and simplicity of the subject. It helps readers connect with nature’s beauty.
  49. Why is the outside world described as “wonderful”?
    Because it is full of beauty and mystery. For the plant, it is a place of discovery.
  50. Why is the poem suitable for children?

Because it uses gentle imagery and teaches about life and growth. It’s easy to understand and full of wonder.
