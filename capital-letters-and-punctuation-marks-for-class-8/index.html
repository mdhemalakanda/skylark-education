  12. Alexander the Great was the king of Macedonia. He was the son of Philip, a mighty king of Sparta. He invaded India about 327 years before the birth of Christ. On the way he defeated Darius and marched towards the Punjab.

  29. One day a farmer took some sacks of wheat to a mill. The mill was far away. On the way the horse stumbled and one of the sacks fell on the ground.
  30. My friend said to me, “Why are you sitting alone in your room at this hour? Don’t you see that the sky is clear?”
